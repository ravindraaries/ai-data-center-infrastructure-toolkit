# Technology Qualification Checklists

Use each question as **Pass / Fail / Not tested / Not applicable**, with a supporting evidence reference. An untested mandatory gate blocks approval.

## Optical compute interconnects
- [ ] Workload bandwidth and latency targets are documented.
- [ ] Electrical baseline is measured under comparable conditions.
- [ ] Reach, signal integrity, and link error performance are qualified.
- [ ] Energy per bit includes optical/electrical conversion overhead.
- [ ] Link redundancy, monitoring, and service procedures are documented.
- [ ] Interoperability with selected compute/network equipment is demonstrated.

## Co-packaged optics testing
- [ ] Optical coupling and insertion-loss acceptance criteria are defined.
- [ ] Transmitter and receiver characterization is documented.
- [ ] Electrical signal integrity and package thermal behavior are tested.
- [ ] Manufacturing yield and test repeatability are measured.
- [ ] Failure diagnosis and field replacement processes are demonstrated.
- [ ] Supplier qualification and reliability evidence are reviewed.

## Liquid-cooled optical transceivers
- [ ] The module is specifically designed for the proposed liquid-cooling architecture.
- [ ] Cooling interface and thermal resistance are qualified.
- [ ] Coolant/material compatibility is confirmed by the supplier.
- [ ] Leak detection, containment, and safe service procedures are documented.
- [ ] Pumping power and cooling-system overhead are measured.
- [ ] Fiber routing, access, and replacement processes are validated.

## In-chip microfluidic cooling
- [ ] Hotspot reduction is demonstrated against an appropriate baseline.
- [ ] Channel geometry, flow distribution, and pressure drop are characterized.
- [ ] Pumping power and total cooling efficiency are measured.
- [ ] Materials, seals, and coolant are qualified for the package.
- [ ] Manufacturing compatibility and reliability testing are documented.
- [ ] Failure modes, monitoring, and containment plans are reviewed.

## Mandatory release gates
- [ ] Applicable safety and compliance requirements met.
- [ ] Acceptance tests completed and traceable.
- [ ] Operations and maintenance team approves procedures.
- [ ] Lifecycle costs and fallback plan reviewed.
- [ ] Open critical risks resolved or explicitly rejected for deployment.
